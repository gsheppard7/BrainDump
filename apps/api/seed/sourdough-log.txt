Sourdough log

Oct 3 — Starter ("Clint") fed 1:5:5 with 20% whole rye. Peaked at 6 hours at 76°F.
Bake #1: 75% hydration, 2% salt, 20% levain. Bulk ferment 5h. Crumb was tight, crust pale.
Probably under-proofed. Oven was only preheated 30 minutes.

Oct 10 — Bake #2: same formula, bulk 6.5h until ~60% rise and jiggly. Cold retard overnight
in the fridge (14h). Preheated the Dutch oven a full hour at 500°F, baked 20 min lid on,
then 22 min lid off at 460°F. Much better oven spring and an ear finally showed up.

Oct 17 — Tried 80% hydration with 10% spelt. Dough was slack and hard to shape. Use more
coil folds early (every 30 min for the first 2 hours). Flavor was great though, nutty.

Oct 24 — Back to 75%. Added a 45-minute autolyse before the levain. Noticeably more
extensible dough. Best loaf so far. Scoring with a single curved cut at 30 degrees works.

Notes to self:
- Water temperature matters more than I thought. Aim for a final dough temp of 78°F.
- Rice flour on the banneton stops sticking completely.
- Next experiment: 15% whole wheat and an overnight bulk at 65°F.
