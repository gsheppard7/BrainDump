# Kyoto trip plan — April

## Logistics
- Flights: SFO → KIX, arrive Thursday afternoon. Haruka Express to Kyoto Station (~75 min).
  Buy the ICOCA card at the airport; it works on buses and the subway.
- Stay: machiya rental near Gojo, check-in after 4pm. Host said bikes are in the back alley,
  lock code is in the house manual (not here).
- Budget target: ¥25,000/day excluding lodging.

## Day-by-day
**Friday** — Fushimi Inari at 7am before the crowds, walk the full loop (2–3 hours).
Lunch at the udon place by the station. Afternoon nap. Evening: Pontocho alley, book a
kaiseki counter if we can get one.

**Saturday** — Arashiyama bamboo grove early, then Okochi Sanso garden (worth the entry fee
for the tea + view). Rent bikes back toward Kitano Tenmangu flea market if it's the 25th.

**Sunday** — Day trip to Nara. Todai-ji, deer park, then Naramachi for coffee.
Return by 5pm — remember Sunday trains get packed.

**Monday** — Philosopher's Path during cherry blossom season, Ginkaku-ji, then Nishiki
Market for gifts: yuzu kosho for Dad, knife sharpening stone for me.

## Things to remember
- Many small restaurants are cash only. Withdraw at 7-Eleven ATMs.
- Temples close around 4:30–5pm.
- Pack a foldable tote — convenience stores charge for bags.
- Ask Kenji for the name of the coffee roaster he recommended in Demachiyanagi.
